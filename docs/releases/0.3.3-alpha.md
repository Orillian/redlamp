Highlights:

- **The Base Looks panel**: Develop's left column has a Base Looks panel before Recipes, with every look in folders (Favorites, In This Photo, the photo's Adobe camera profiles, Redlamp, Film Styles, Film Stocks with their icons, Firmware Model while it's on, Fujifilm LUTs, Imported and Reproduction), its own search, hover to preview and click to apply. A film stock, wherever it's chosen, sets its look with its grain, halation and bloom and leaves the rest of Effects as it was. Recipes lists only presets, and a recipe that sets a Base Look shows it on its row. Your own and installed recipes and imported looks have Show in Finder and Move to Trash, in place of Delete Recipe, and a built-in folder can be hidden from either panel, its search and ⌘K, then shown again from the panel header's menu; photos keep the looks they use ([#469](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/469), [#470](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/470)).
- **Lightroom edits brought across**: File › Import from Lightroom Classic… opens on what it finds on the Mac (the catalog Lightroom Classic last used, the presets in Camera Raw's Settings folder, and the Adobe camera and lens profiles) and brings each across on its own. A catalog's photo edits are read as data and converted once, without changing the catalog; Library › Convert Lightroom Edits…, and opening such a photo, do the same for Lightroom's, Camera Raw's and Bridge's XMP, in sidecars or inside DNG, JPEG, TIFF, HEIC and PNG files, and Redlamp never writes Lightroom's XMP. Each comes with Undo, and a photo already edited in Redlamp keeps its edit. Raws render in the photographer's own Adobe profile where it's on the Mac, and otherwise in Redlamp's look of the same name. A report says what came across and what was left out, with the reason: masks, spots, Upright's modes and panel switches stay out until they're checked against Lightroom's own files ([#312](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/312), [#74](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/74), [#434](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/434), [#435](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/435)).
- **Your own Adobe camera profiles**: for a raw, the Base Look menu and the Base Looks panel list the Adobe camera profiles on the Mac for its camera (Adobe Raw, Camera Matching, Adobe's creative groups and your own), read where Lightroom Classic and Camera Raw install them, or from files chosen with Choose Camera Profiles…, and never copied. Adobe's looks go over their camera's Adobe Standard at the Base Look's Amount, from 0 to 200. Nothing of a profile goes into an edit's file, a recipe or an exported LUT: where a profile isn't on the Mac, Redlamp's look of the same name stands in, with a notice. They have been checked with test profiles so far, not yet with Adobe's own ([#432](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/432), [#433](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/433)).
