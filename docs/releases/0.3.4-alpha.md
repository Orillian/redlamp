Highlights:

- **Import from Apple Photos**: File › Import from Apple Photos… lists the System Photo Library's albums, folders and smart albums with their counts, and copies the chosen albums' originals out through PhotoKit, downloading those kept only in iCloud, to Import's destination by its folder and name templates, which gain an `{album}` token. A RAW+JPEG photo comes as a pair, ProRAW as its DNG, a Live Photo as its still, and a burst as its key photo and picked frames, stacked; videos are left out and counted. A photo edited in Photos comes as its original with Photos' rendered version stacked over it. Favourites become picks, original names and dates changed in Photos come across, albums become collections and Photos' folders collection sets, and photos already imported are skipped. A report says what comes across and what stays in Photos (titles, people, places set only in Photos) before anything is copied, and the import has Stop and Undo Import. The albums chosen are followed: their new photos are offered in the Library panel's From Apple Photos, and Settings › Library's Import new photos from followed albums automatically, off by default, imports them as they arrive. Photos is only read, never changed. Ratings, captions and keywords wait on a build with the macOS 27 SDK, and the import has been tried on a simulated library so far, not yet on a real one ([#488](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/488), [#489](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/489)).
- **Zoom out past Fit**: the wheel and a pinch zoom out past Fit to a quarter of it, the photo centred on the stage at its own shape. Zooming out holds at Fit first, for three notches of the wheel or one of a pinch, so it's easy to stop there; zooming in passes Fit without holding. ⌘- steps past Fit to a half and a quarter of it, and ⌘= back. Photos still open at Fit, Z and a click still switch between Fit and 1:1, and the Library's Loupe keeps its own Fit and 1:1 ([#479](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/479)).
