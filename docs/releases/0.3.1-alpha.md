Highlights:

- **JPEG XL export**: the Export dialog writes JPEG XL, under both Lossy and Lossless in its Format menu, in 8 or 16 bits and in sRGB or Display P3, with the same metadata and embedded edit as the other formats; `redlamp` on the command line writes `.jxl` too ([#373](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/373)).
- **The filmstrip**: a scroll bar to cross a long folder in one drag; thumbnails in three sizes, Small, Medium and Large, or any height between by dragging the filmstrip's top edge; Home and End for the first and last photo; its line of text hidden by a setting; and a pin at the end of that line that keeps the filmstrip shown, turning Hide Automatically off and on ([#376](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/376), [#396](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/396)).
- **Looks in one place**: the Base Look menu, its browser and ⌘K list only the looks that aren't recipes, with the photo's own look first; film looks are recipes in the Recipes panel, with film icons, where Apply Base Look Only previews a look on its own while it's highlighted; and recipe search finds Base Looks too ([#404](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/404)).
