Highlights:

- **Batch export**: Export… and Export with Previous act on every photo selected in the filmstrip or the grid, the dialog saying how many, and render them one at a time in the background, with progress and Cancel. Files are named by the same templates as Rename Photos and Import, with sequences in the filmstrip's order, and a photo already in the folder is never replaced; for earlier exports, Ask asks once for the whole batch, and Skip leaves them as they are. `redlamp export` does the same on the command line ([#78](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/78)).
- **Red, green and blue curves**: in the Tone Curve's Point mode, a Channel menu (RGB, Red, Green, Blue) picks the curve the graph edits, as Lightroom Classic's point curve has, each channel's curve drawn in its colour over its histogram ([#379](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/379)).
- **Fujifilm film simulations from the camera's own processing, as an experiment**: turn on Firmware film model in Settings › Experimental and Redlamp downloads a model of the X100VI's own processing, recovered from its firmware, whose 20 film simulations the Camera Recipe group in Develop's Effects offers; it's off by default and may be withdrawn, but the looks your photos use stay on your Mac. The model is fitted and checked against raw and JPEG pairs, so yours help: the RAF and the camera's own JPEG of a frame, shot RAW+JPEG and unedited, from an X100VI first and other X-Trans cameras too, and Soft Slide and Cinema above all. Post a link to them on [the samples issue](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/448) ([#428](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/428)).
