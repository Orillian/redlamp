Highlights:

- **Batch export**: Export… and Export with Previous act on every photo selected in the filmstrip or the grid, the dialog saying how many, and render them one at a time in the background, with progress and Cancel. Files are named by the same templates as Rename Photos and Import, with sequences in the filmstrip's order, and a file already there is never replaced: Ask asks once for the whole batch, or Skip leaves it. `redlamp export` does the same on the command line ([#78](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/78)).
- **Red, green and blue curves**: in the Tone Curve's Point mode, a Channel menu (RGB, Red, Green, Blue) picks the curve the graph edits, as Lightroom Classic's point curve has, each channel's curve drawn in its colour over its histogram ([#379](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/379)).
- **Fujifilm film simulations from the camera's own processing, as an experiment**: with Settings' Experimental switch on, Fujifilm's film simulations come from a model of the X100VI's own processing, recovered from its firmware, downloaded when you turn it on ([#428](https://github.com/pdcgomes/redlamp/issues/428)).
