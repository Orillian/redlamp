## 6. What's left out

- Location and serial numbers are never read into the report: Redlamp doesn't read them into its own types, and this never asks ImageIO for them (`FeedbackContext.swift:8-9`).
- Aliases: "Photo A", "Photo B" … "Photo Z", "Photo AA", by the order photos were first opened this session (`ActivityLog.swift:120-146`).
- Redaction of the issue's title and body and of `diagnostics.json`: each known folder (the open folder and the folders the person added, `FeedbackContext.swift:122`; `FolderLibrary.swift:90-91`) becomes "…/" before what's inside it, or "a folder"; the known folders include where Redlamp keeps photos kept in Apple Photos, in its cache and their sidecars on this Mac, and the active photo's own folders there (`FeedbackContext.swift:143, 290-305`); then the active photo's Photos identifiers (its local and cloud identifiers, its key in Redlamp's cache, and a copy's asset as its sidecar names it) become "…" (`FeedbackContext.swift:306-320`; `FeedbackRedaction.swift:51-53`); the home folder becomes "~"; any other path of two or more parts becomes "…/<last part>"; the titles of the albums kept in Apple Photos and of Photos' folders holding them, Photos Chosen for Redlamp aside, become "an album" and "a folder" wherever one of three characters or more stands whole (`FeedbackContext.swift:282-289`; `FeedbackRedaction.swift:69-71, 89-111`); unless File names is on, each photo's file name, and its name without the extension, becomes its alias (`FeedbackRedaction.swift:44-87`; tests `FeedbackReportTests.swift:125-133, 322-366`, `ApplePhotosReportTests.swift`).
- The body includes the person's own words, so they're redacted too (`FeedbackReport.swift:189-194`). So is the title, by the same redactor with the same File names choice, before it's cut to 120 characters; the area in brackets before it is left as it is (`FeedbackReport.swift:149-154`). The preview, the submission, the outbox and Your Reports' sent reports all have the redacted title (`FeedbackSheetModel.swift:150-152, 190-195`; `FeedbackClient.swift:138-141`; `FeedbackHistory.swift:191-202, 254-262`; tests `FeedbackSheetTests.swift:141-154`, `FeedbackHistoryTests.swift:75-107`). The relay only escapes HTML comments in it (`feedback.ts:136, 148-150`).
- The photo itself is never attached: the attachments are the screenshots and `diagnostics.json` (`FeedbackClient.swift:122-143`).

## 13. Crash and hang reports

- Each session keeps a journal in Application Support/Redlamp/Feedback/Session: the activity, the photos opened, the folders shown and the hangs, each line written as it happens, and a last line once it quits; it keeps the latest 500 events (`SessionJournal.swift:5-10, 109-116, 140-149, 268-269`).
- The hang watchdog marks the main thread away from its run loop for 2 s or more, checking every second, while it lasts and with its length once it's back; its clock stops while the Mac sleeps (`HangWatchdog.swift:6-11, 45-47`). A hang it came back from is written into the activity as "Not responding for 14 s" (`SessionRecorder.swift:48-55`).
- What the next launch offers, in order (`IncidentCheck.swift:41-79`):
  1. A crash, from macOS's crash report (`~/Library/Logs/DiagnosticReports`, `MacCrashReport.swift:3-10`) merged with MetricKit's (`IncidentCheck.swift:81-92`).
  2. Else, if the session didn't quit and the Mac didn't restart since, the hang it ended in, as a Force Quit of a stuck Redlamp ends it: `endedSession` (`:62-65`; `Incident.swift:56-57`).
  3. Else the longest hang of 10 s or more, however the session ended, after a restart too (`IncidentCheck.swift:47-48, 66-69`; `IncidentCheckTests.swift:33-52`).
  4. Else nothing: a Force Quit of a Redlamp that was responding, or the Mac restarting during the session, offers nothing (`IncidentCheck.swift:41-45`; `IncidentCheckTests.swift:54-59`).
  A session that ended without quitting and has no crash report yet waits 6 s for one (`IncidentCheck.swift:49-51, 117-119`).
- Only sessions of the same bundle ID and the same kind of build are read, and their journals are removed once read (`IncidentCheck.swift:97-128`).
- Development builds (Debug and Profiling, `RedlampApp.swift:183-189`) keep and offer what's found only with the defaults key `feedback.offersIncidentReports`, since Xcode's Stop button and the regression suite end them without quitting (`SessionRecorder.swift:10-12, 41, 81-85`; `RedlampApp.swift:90-96`).
- The banner: over the photo in Develop, below a damaged edit's and a failed save's banners (`CanvasArea.swift:105-111`); in the Library, over the top of the photos, while no failed save or culling problem is shown in its place (`IncidentBanner.swift:44-50`; `LibraryModuleView.swift:23-25, 70`). It shows "Redlamp quit unexpectedly" or "Redlamp stopped responding" (`Incident.swift:72-75`) and "Today at 10:08. You can read the report before you send it.", a hang's with ", for 14 s" after the time (`IncidentBanner.swift:52-65`). **Review Report…** (help "Opens the report, to read before you send it") and **Not Now** (help "Keeps it in Help › Your Reports for 30 days") (`IncidentBanner.swift:26-33`).
- The banner offers the newest crash or hang not dismissed (`FeedbackHistory.swift:156-170`). ## A15. The command line (`LibraryCommand+Photos.swift`)

- `redlamp library photos` reads Photos through PhotoKit, only ever reading it, and asks macOS for access the first time (`LibraryCommand+Photos.swift:6-19, 76-83`). It takes one of `--albums`, `--census`, `--apply`, `--undo`, `--keep`, `--list`, `--originals` and `--evict`, else "photos takes one of --albums, --census, --apply, --undo, --keep, --list, --originals and --evict" (`:53-58`). It exits 1 when Redlamp may not read Photos, or when an import or Undo left a photo out (`:43-45, 80-83, 207, 321, 339`).
- `--albums`: Photos' albums, folders, smart albums and shared albums, each folder's indented below it, with Photos' estimate and its ID, kinds printed as album, smart album, "shared album, reduced copies" and folder (`:19-20, 140-155`).
- `--census`: what importing the albums `--album` names would bring into the library at `--index`, written nowhere and naming no album, photo or place (`:20-26`).
- `--apply --destination <folder>`: the import, files staged in a hidden folder at the destination, then copied in folders and names from `--folders` and `--names` (`--folders {album}` puts each photo in a folder named after its first album), checked and indexed; it prints "Imported N from Apple Photos as M photos, checked, to <path>: <state>; redlamp library photos --undo takes it back" (`:26-30, 196-198`). Without `--destination`: "--apply needs --destination, the folder the photos go in" (`:116-118`).
- `--undo`: takes the last import back, its copies to the Trash: "Took back the import from Apple Photos of N photos: M files moved to the Trash" (`:30-31, 332-333`).
- `--keep [--album …]`: keeps those albums in Photos beside those kept already, copying nothing, their photos indexed from what Photos gives, Redlamp's edits for them kept on this Mac; without `--album` it indexes the albums kept again. It prints "Kept N albums in place: P photos from Apple Photos, …", the copies that stand in ("N of them copied into the library before: …") and "left out: N videos, N hidden photos, N burst frames not picked" (`:31-35, 88-97, 212-243`).
- `--list`: the albums kept, with the photos the library has of each, from the index alone, or "No album is kept in place: redlamp library photos --keep --album <title> keeps one" (`:35-36, 245-272`).
- `--originals`: "Originals downloaded from iCloud: N, <size> of a budget of <size>, in <folder>"; `--evict` empties the cache first, "Took N originals out of the cache, <size>", the originals downloaded again when next needed (`:36-38, 274-305`).
- `--album`: a title, a path of titles such as `Trips/Lisbon`, or an ID, repeatable; a folder for its albums; without one, every album but smart and shared albums; an unknown one gives "Photos has no album <name>: --albums lists them" (`:20-22, 88-97, 128-137`; `ApplePhotosLibrary.swift:451-462`). `--include-hidden` brings hidden photos; `--index <path>` names the library, the app's own without it; `--simulated <manifest>` reads a simulated library instead of Photos; `--json` prints JSON (`:22-23, 31, 39-40, 63-65`).
- No command copies photos kept in Photos (Copy to Folder), removes one, or stops keeping an album **(inferred: the modes, `:53`)**. `redlamp library --help`'s line for photos names only `--apply` and `--undo`, and points to `redlamp library photos --help` (`LibraryCommand.swift:204-205`). `redlamp export` doesn't yet export photos kept in Photos (`docs/research/research-tracker.md:690`).
