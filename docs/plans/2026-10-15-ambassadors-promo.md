# The ambassadors promo

The recruitment reel and carousel for Redlamp's ambassador programme (DEC-86): ten founding photographers who pick one feature a quarter that's built next, and whose edits are shown with their name. The programme, its terms and its phases are in the private social repository (`plans/2026-10-15-ambassadors.md`); the campaign's visual system is in its marketing library (`marketing/campaigns/ambassadors/`). Made in the promo studio (`.cursor/skills/redlamp-promo-studio/SKILL.md`), and posted through the social room.

## Brief

| Row | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| Goal | Applications to the ambassador programme at redlamp.app/join: none on 15 October 2026 (the form isn't live yet); 30 by the open call's close, three weeks after it opens. |
| Audience | Photographers on the Mac who edit raw and show their edits: Lightroom users, people who follow Fujifilm-style recipes, and the people already editing in Redlamp. |
| Platforms | 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Stories; a 4:5 carousel for the Instagram feed. |
| Length | 8 bars at 100 BPM (18 frames a beat at 30 fps): 19.2 seconds. |

| Tone | Calm and cinematic. What happens can be playful; the words stay plain, and the music dark and sophisticated (the owner's taste). |

## Concept

Three were considered:

1. **The print comes up** (chosen). A blank sheet of paper under the lamp. On the drop, a photographer's edit develops on it, the way a print comes up in the developer, and the credit strip lands with their handle: your edits, on Redlamp's feed. Cut to the roadmap, where one row lifts into the lamp's light: you pick what's built next. Then the contact sheet of ten founding places, the ones left ringed in white marker. Then the ask.
2. **The empty frame.** The contact sheet first, the camera pushing into the one empty frame, which fills with the viewer's photo. Strong for the founding places, weaker on the say in the roadmap.
3. **The seat.** The roadmap list first, the pick lifting, then the edits. It leads with the most unusual part of the offer, but a list is a weak first frame in a feed.

The print comes up wins because it opens on the darkroom, which is the brand's own world, makes the photographer's work the hero from the first second, and its develop is a small physical event that holds attention to the drop. Its four beats are the studio's: tension (the blank sheet, the lamp charging), release (the print comes up on the drop), payoff (the credit, then the pick), the ask.

## Hooks

Each is a prop of the composition (`hook`), rendered and posted as its own variant. On screen from the first frame, over the blank sheet under the lamp. After the copy review (`work/ambassadors/copy-review-1.md` in the social repository), `pick` leads and `feed` is the trial reel's: `pick` says what the viewer gets in five words, and it's the one thing no other editor offers.

| ID | Hook | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pick` | PICK WHAT REDLAMP / BUILDS NEXT | Leads: the most unusual part of the offer, and it names Redlamp in the first frame |
| `feed` | YOUR EDITS ON / REDLAMP'S FEED | The trial reel's: the broader pull for photographers |
| `founding` | 10 FOUNDING / AMBASSADORS | Scarcity that's true, once read with the places left |
| `become` | BECOME A REDLAMP / AMBASSADOR | The plain name of the thing |
| `photographers` | WE NEED / PHOTOGRAPHERS | Calls the audience by name, but puts Redlamp's need first; kept as a variant |

A variant whose hook is a later line says it again there (`pick` at beat 12, `feed` at beat 8, `founding` at beat 16). For `pick` the repeat lands as the row lifts, which works; the others are the owner's call.

## Script

8 bars at 100 BPM, in D minor. Beats count from 0; a beat is 0.6 seconds. In the reel, the roadmap and the contact sheet are drawn without their own headings and APPLY line, so the line on screen is the heading and no more than two lines show at once; YOUR PICK and the marker's places left stay, as part of the picture.

| Beat | Time | Picture | Words on screen | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0.0 s | The blank sheet under the lamp; its glow breathing | The hook | A low drone and a soft pulse from the first frame |
| 4 | 2.4 s | The sheet settles in the tray; the light gathers behind the lamp | A FREE RAW EDITOR / FOR THE MAC | A riser starts; a felt piano's first notes |
| 7.5 | 4.5 s | The breath: the glow dims for half a beat | | Everything stops but a swell |
| 8 | 4.8 s | The drop: the photo comes up out of white over two beats | YOUR EDITS ON / REDLAMP'S FEED | The groove arrives; a paper wash under the develop |
| 10 | 6.0 s | The credit strip slides up: the mark, the handle, EDITED IN REDLAMP | (held) | A small tick as the strip lands |
| 12 | 7.2 s | Cut on the bar: the roadmap list; on beat 13 one row lifts into the lamp's light, YOUR PICK | PICK WHAT REDLAMP / BUILDS NEXT | The chord moves; a low lift under the row |
| 16 | 9.6 s | The contact sheet of ten frames: four fill, one a beat; on beat 19 the marker rings the places left | 10 FOUNDING / AMBASSADORS | A shutter click a frame; the marker's squeak |
| 20 | 12.0 s | Back to the lamp and a fresh sheet | EDIT A PHOTO / IN REDLAMP | The groove thins |
| 24 | 14.4 s | The end card, under the lamp | APPLY AT / REDLAMP.APP/JOIN | A last hit, then the tail |
| 30 | 18.0 s | The fresh sheet, which loops into the first frame | | The tail dies away by beat 32 |

Every line is on screen at least 2.4 seconds (four words take about 1.7 to read); the ask, 3.6. The row that lifts is one that could be on a quarter's shortlist (Accepted, Not started, in the phase, S or M): the Targeted Adjustment Tool (UX-35) as drawn, which the owner may change. The places left are read from the cohort on the day the reel is rendered.

## Formats

- **9:16, 1080 × 1920**, for Reels, TikTok and Stories: the sheet and the words in the middle, clear of the apps' safe zones (`editing.md`).
- **4:5, 1080 × 1350**, for the feed: the same, framed tighter.
- **The carousel**, seven slides at 4:5 (below).

## Sound

D minor at 100 BPM: a sub pulse and a dark pad from the first frame, a felt piano from beat 4, a low drum and a plucked string on the drop, the chord moving on the bar. Sounds on the events: a paper wash under the develop, a tick for the strip, a lift for the pick, a shutter click for each frame, a marker's squeak for the ring, a last hit on the end card. Original, from `video/scripts/synth.py`, mastered to the platforms' loudness.

## The carousel

Seven slides at 4:5, made by `marketing/campaigns/ambassadors/build.py` (`carousel`). Every photo is Redlamp's render of the owner's raw (the colour hunt's renders, `work/colour-hunt/prototype/tiles/`), so EDITED IN REDLAMP is true.

1. **Cover:** an edit with the credit strip. BECOME A REDLAMP / AMBASSADOR.
2. **The pick:** the roadmap pick, YOUR PICK on the lifted row. PICK WHAT REDLAMP / BUILDS NEXT. "Each quarter, ambassadors pick one feature from a shortlist of my roadmap, and I build it next."
3. **The credit:** an edit with the credit strip. YOUR EDITS ON / REDLAMP'S FEED. "Always with your name and a link to you."
4. **Posts made for you:** a raw as Redlamp opens it beside the same raw in a camera recipe. POSTS MADE / FOR YOU. "Send me a raw and its edit, and I send back a before-and-after carousel and reel to post as your own."
5. **Founding places:** the contact sheet, ten frames. 10 FOUNDING / AMBASSADORS. "The first ten are listed on redlamp.app as founding ambassadors, for good."
6. **What I ask:** ONE EDIT / A MONTH. "Send me one edit a month, and tell me what gets in your way. It's unpaid, and needs an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later."
7. **The ask:** APPLY AT / REDLAMP.APP/JOIN. "Edit a photo in Redlamp, then apply with it."

## For the owner to decide

- **The script and the hooks** (studio checkpoint 1), before the reel is built: `pick` to lead and `feed` for the trial reel, as the copy review recommends.
- **The end card** `APPLY AT / REDLAMP.APP/JOIN` in place of the standard `DOWNLOAD FREE / REDLAMP.APP`, for this campaign; the copy review would make the exception, since EDIT A PHOTO / IN REDLAMP comes just before it.
- **The row that lifts:** the Targeted Adjustment Tool as drawn, or another row he'd shortlist.
- **Whose photo develops, and the handle on it:** one of the owner's Redlamp renders and his Instagram handle until there's an ambassador, then theirs, with their permission.
- **"macOS 26" in posts.json's `requirements`:** to "macOS 26 or later", as the README and the page say.
