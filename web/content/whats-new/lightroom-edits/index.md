File › Import from Lightroom Classic… finds the catalog Lightroom last used, Camera Raw's presets and your Adobe profiles, and brings each across. The catalog is never changed, and Library › Convert Lightroom Edits… reads Lightroom's, Camera Raw's and Bridge's XMP sidecars without writing them.

Each photo converts once, and one you've edited in Redlamp keeps its edit. Raws render in your own Adobe profile where it's on the Mac. A report names what's left out and why: masks, spots, Upright's modes and panel switches wait until they're checked against Lightroom's own files.
