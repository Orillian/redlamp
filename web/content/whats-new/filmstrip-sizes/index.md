Choose Small, Medium or Large thumbnails in View › Filmstrip, the filmstrip's right-click menu or Settings › Appearance, or drag the filmstrip's top edge to any height between. A scroll bar crosses a long folder in one drag, and Home and End (fn-← and fn-→ on a laptop) go to the first and last photo.

In Develop, the pin at the end of the filmstrip's line of text keeps it shown, with the photo fitted above it: a click turns Hide Automatically off, and another turns it back on. Turn off Show Info Line, beside Hide Automatically, to hide the line.
