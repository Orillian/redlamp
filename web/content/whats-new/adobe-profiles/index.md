For a raw, the Base Look menu and the Base Looks panel list the Adobe profiles on your Mac for its camera: Adobe Raw, Camera Matching, Adobe's creative groups and your own, read where Lightroom Classic and Camera Raw install them. Choose Camera Profiles… adds others. Adobe's looks take the Amount, from 0 to 200.

Redlamp never copies a profile or puts one in an edit, recipe or LUT: where it isn't on the Mac, Redlamp's look of the same name stands in, with a notice. They're tried with test files so far, so tell us if one looks different from Lightroom.
