File › Import from Apple Photos… lists your albums, folders and smart albums with their counts, and copies the originals in the ones you choose into your folders. Favourites become picks and albums collections; a photo edited in Photos comes with Photos' version stacked over it. A report says what comes across before anything is copied, and Undo Import takes it back.

Photos is only read, never changed. New photos in those albums are offered in the Library panel. It's been tried on a simulated library so far, so tell us how it goes with yours.
