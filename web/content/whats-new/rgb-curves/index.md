In Point mode, a Channel menu under the Tone Curve's Curve menu picks the curve the graph edits: RGB, Red, Green or Blue, as in Lightroom Classic's point curve. Each channel's curve is drawn in its colour over that channel's histogram, with the other bent curves faint behind it, and applies after the RGB curve.

Copy Settings, Sync and recipes carry the channels' curves, and Lightroom presets' red, green and blue point curves are now read too.
