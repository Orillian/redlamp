Each film stock is now listed once, as a recipe in the Recipes panel's Film Stocks folder, with its film's icon. Right-click a film recipe for Apply Base Look Only, which previews its look on its own while the item is highlighted. The panel's search finds Base Looks too, after the recipes.

The Base Look menu, its browser and the ⌘K list show only the looks that aren't recipes, with the photo's own look first.
