Turn on Firmware film model in Settings › Experimental, and Redlamp downloads a model of the X100VI's own processing, recovered from its firmware, with 20 film simulations for the Camera Recipe group in Develop's Effects. It's checked on few photos so far and may be withdrawn, but the looks your photos use stay on your Mac.

Raw and JPEG pairs help fit it: the RAF and the camera's own JPEG of a frame, unedited, from an X100VI first, in any simulation, Soft Slide and Cinema above all. Post a link to yours on the samples issue; they're used only to fit Redlamp's looks, never published.
