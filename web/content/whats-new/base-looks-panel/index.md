Develop's left column has a Base Looks panel before Recipes, with every look in folders: your Adobe camera profiles, Redlamp's looks, the Film Styles, the Film Stocks with their icons, your LUTs. It has its own search; hover to preview, click to apply. A film stock brings its grain, halation and bloom and leaves the rest of Effects as it was. Recipes now lists only presets.

Your own recipes and imported looks have Show in Finder and Move to Trash, and a built-in folder can be hidden from its right-click menu. Photos keep the looks they use.
